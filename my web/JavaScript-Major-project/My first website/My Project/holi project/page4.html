celebration of holi

Holi is celebrated with great excitement all over india. On this day,people throw colored powder and colored water on each other.
Children enjoy playing with water ballons and water guns,

People also prepare special swwets such as Gujiya and drinks like Thandai. Famalies and friends visit each other and share swets and greetings.

Holi teaches us the message of love,unity,and happiness. it brings people together and spread joy everywhere.

conclusion:
"Holi is a beautiful festival that fills our lives with colors,happiness,and friendship."
