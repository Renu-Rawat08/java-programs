Introduction of Holi

Holi is one of the popular festival in india. It is kwown as the festival of colors.People celebrate this festival with great joy and enthusiasm every year in the month of March.

On this day,people apply colors to each other,dance, sings songs,and enjoy with friends and family.Holi brings happiness and spread love anf friendship among people.

This festival also marks the arrival of the spring season. It reminds us to forget our difference and live peace and harmony.
