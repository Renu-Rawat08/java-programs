Story of Holi

The festival of Holi is connected with a famous story from Hindu mythology.Long ago there was a king named Hiranyakashipa. He was very proud and wanted everyone to worship him.

But his son Prahlad was a great devotee of Vishnu .This made the king very angry.

Hiranyakashipa asked his sister Holika to sit in fire with Prahlad because she had a magiacal cloth that protected her from fire.but due to Prahlad's devotion,he saved and Holika was burned in the fire.

This event is rememnered as Holika Dahan, which is celebrated one day before Holi. It shows that good always win over evil.
